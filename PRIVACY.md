# ChatGPT Timekeeper privacy policy

Updated October 11, 2026. Developer: AlexS778.

## Drafts and timestamps

The extension reads and edits the current text draft on chatgpt.com to add your device's date, time and GMT offset. Draft text is handled temporarily in memory; the extension does not save prompts, responses or chat history.

When you choose Send, the timestamp and your message are sent to ChatGPT through the website's normal submission. The GMT offset can suggest a broad time-zone region, but is not a GPS location. Your device clock may be inaccurate. ChatGPT's own data handling applies to messages you send.

## Local settings and control

Only the enabled on/off preference is saved in local extension storage, without extension-managed cloud sync. Turn off “Automatically add time” in the popup to stop adding timestamps. Removing the extension clears its local preference; it does not delete messages already sent to ChatGPT. Use ChatGPT's controls for those messages.

## No developer collection

The extension makes no network requests, sends no data to its developer, and has no analytics, advertising, telemetry or remote code. Data is not sold, used for unrelated purposes or used for credit or lending decisions. Its handling of user data complies with the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
